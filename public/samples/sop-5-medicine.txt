Target University: Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health

Dear Admissions Committee,

The day a measles outbreak claimed three children in my village changed the trajectory of my life. As a 12-year-old in rural Egypt, I witnessed preventable deaths that sparked my lifelong commitment to public health. Today, with five years of clinical experience and a deep understanding of healthcare disparities, I am ready to translate that commitment into systemic change.

MEDICAL EDUCATION AND CLINICAL EXPERIENCE
I earned my MD from Cairo University Faculty of Medicine, graduating in the top 5% of my class. During medical school, I volunteered with Doctors Without Borders in South Sudan, an experience that profoundly shaped my understanding of healthcare in resource-limited settings.

After completing my residency in Internal Medicine at the American University of Beirut Medical Center, I joined the World Health Organization's Eastern Mediterranean Regional Office. For the past three years, I have worked on polio eradication efforts across Egypt, Pakistan, and Afghanistan, coordinating vaccination campaigns that reached over 2 million children.

WHY HARVARD CHAN?
Harvard's MPH program offers the perfect combination of epidemiological rigor and policy focus that my career requires. The school's emphasis on global health, combined with its proximity to major policy institutions, provides an unparalleled platform for learning.

I am particularly drawn to the work of Dr. Ashish Jha on health systems strengthening and Dr. Margaret Kruk's research on high-quality health systems in low-income countries. Their work directly informs my professional interests.

CAREER GOALS
Following my MPH, I plan to return to the WHO with enhanced skills in health policy and epidemiology. My long-term vision is to lead the development of resilient primary healthcare systems across the Middle East and North Africa region.

I believe that my clinical background, field experience, and passion for equity make me a strong candidate for Harvard Chan's MPH program.

With gratitude,
Dr. Ahmed Hassan, MD
