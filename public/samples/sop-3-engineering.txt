Dear Admissions Committee,

Growing up in Mexico City, I witnessed firsthand how renewable energy could transform communities. When solar panels were installed in my grandmother's rural village, enabling refrigeration for the first time, I knew I wanted to dedicate my career to sustainable engineering.

ACADEMIC FOUNDATION
I completed my Bachelor's in Mechanical Engineering at Universidad Nacional Autonoma de Mexico (UNAM) with honors, achieving a GPA of 3.75/4.0. My coursework emphasized thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, and energy systems. My bachelor's thesis, "Optimization of Wind Turbine Blade Design for Low Wind Speed Regions," received the university's Outstanding Thesis Award.

RESEARCH EXPERIENCE
For the past two years, I have worked as a Research Engineer at the Mexican Institute of Petroleum's Renewable Energy Division. My team developed a novel solar thermal collector design that achieved 15% higher efficiency than conventional models. This work resulted in two patent applications and a publication in the Journal of Renewable Energy.

WHY TUM?
Germany is at the forefront of the global energy transition, and TUM's Department of Mechanical Engineering is renowned for its cutting-edge research in renewable energy systems. I am particularly interested in the work of Professor Thomas Hamacher at the Institute for Renewable and Sustainable Energy Systems.

TUM's strong industry connections with companies like Siemens and BMW offer invaluable opportunities for practical application of research. Additionally, studying in Germany will allow me to learn from a country that has successfully integrated over 40% renewable energy into its grid.

CAREER OBJECTIVES
Upon completing my master's degree, I aim to return to Latin America and lead the development of renewable energy infrastructure. My long-term goal is to establish a research center focused on adapting clean energy technologies for developing nations.

I am excited about the possibility of contributing to TUM's research community while preparing to make a meaningful impact on global sustainability.

Sincerely,
Maria Rodriguez
