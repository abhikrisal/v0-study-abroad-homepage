HONORS THESIS
================================================================================

Title: "Digital Disruption in Southeast Asian Telecommunications: A Case Study 
        of Competitive Dynamics and Strategic Responses"
Advisor: Prof. Michael Tay, Department of Economics
Status: Completed with Distinction
Grade: A+ (98/100)

Summary: Conducted detailed econometric analysis of the telecommunications 
sector in Southeast Asia, examining how digital disruption has impacted market 
structure, firm competitiveness, and consumer welfare. The thesis utilized 
panel data analysis and dynamic modeling techniques to forecast industry trends 
and corporate strategy implications.

Key Findings:
- Identified key drivers of market consolidation in regional telecom sector
- Quantified consumer welfare gains from digital competition (estimated 8-12%)
- Provided strategic recommendations for incumbent operators

Publication: The thesis was selected for presentation at the Asian Economics 
Association conference and is under consideration for journal publication.
