Dear Admissions Committee,

At 28, I have spent the last five years building a fintech startup that now serves over 200,000 users across Southeast Asia. Yet, I find myself at a crossroads where I need the strategic thinking and leadership skills that only a world-class MBA program can provide.

PROFESSIONAL JOURNEY
After graduating from National University of Singapore with a degree in Economics, I joined Goldman Sachs as an analyst in their Investment Banking Division. Those two years taught me the mechanics of finance, but more importantly, they showed me the inefficiencies in how financial services reached everyday consumers.

In 2021, I co-founded PayEasy, a mobile payment platform targeting the unbanked population in Indonesia and the Philippines. Within three years, we raised $15M in Series A funding, grew our team to 85 employees, and processed over $500M in transactions. Leading this venture taught me invaluable lessons about product development, team building, and navigating regulatory challenges.

WHY STANFORD GSB?
Stanford's emphasis on "Change Lives. Change Organizations. Change the World" resonates deeply with my entrepreneurial mission. The collaborative culture, combined with access to Silicon Valley's ecosystem, makes Stanford the ideal place to refine my vision for PayEasy's expansion.

I am particularly drawn to Professor Ilya Strebulaev's research on venture capital and entrepreneurial finance, as well as the opportunity to participate in the Stanford Venture Studio.

FUTURE VISION
Post-MBA, I plan to scale PayEasy across all of ASEAN, ultimately serving 50 million users by 2030. I believe financial inclusion is the key to lifting millions out of poverty, and Stanford GSB will equip me with the tools to make this vision a reality.

Thank you for considering my application.
