Abstract:
This dissertation presents a comprehensive analysis of machine learning methods
for predicting 30-day hospital readmissions using NHS patient data. The study
compared traditional statistical approaches with modern ensemble methods,
demonstrating that a stacked ensemble model achieved 92% accuracy with
significant improvements in sensitivity for high-risk patient identification.
